# PaidHR brand: tone and key phrases

From PaidHR's own copy only. Source: `context/docs/research/voice.md` unless stated.

## Tone of voice

| Channel | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Website, LinkedIn | Plain, benefit-led, short claims; leans on hard numbers | "Payroll that never misses payday" |
| X | Playful Nigerian internet humour, wordplay on the name | "Were you paid or were you 𝑷𝒂𝒅𝒆??" |
| Everywhere | Speaks to employers (control, compliance) and employees (wellbeing) at once | "Control for the business. Wellbeing for the people." |
| Sign-off | Blue heart emoji 💙 on most posts; CTA "Book a demo" | LinkedIn posts |

Tone readings are INFERRED in voice.md.
