# PaidHR buyer personas

Built only from `context/docs/research/`. Customer quotes come from YouTube auto-captions (customer-videos.md); check each against the video before using it in copy. [Brackets] = caption corrections.

## 1. Champion: HR operations lead

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Job titles | HR Operations Specialist (caption unclear), HR Manager, People Operations | customer-videos.md: Heroshe; signals.md: 4 |
| Real example | Michelle Adaware, Heroshe | customer-videos.md |
| Measured on | Implied by research: statutory deductions remitted on time with proof, no tax office calls, time for strategic work | customer-videos.md: Heroshe |
| Starts looking when | Remittances take weeks and the tax office keeps calling | customer-videos.md: Heroshe |

**Top three pains, own words**
1. "the major HR problem or challenge I faced in a couple of years will be remitting statutory deductions"
2. "you have to wait for two weeks or more to get that processed and sometimes you keep getting calls from the tax office"
3. "every single time when I meet the employees to get their pays ... they always reach out to me and I have to do the manual process"

**Likely objections** (from research)
| Objection | Evidence | Response proof |
|---|---|---|
| "Our HR software doesn't do remittances, why would yours?" | Heroshe: most HR software can't | Receipts as proof of payment (Heroshe); "₦934M in PAYE filed" in 2025 (company.md) |
| Is it updated for the new tax law? | Old bands give wrong deductions (market.md) | "Fully updated for Nigeria's new tax law, effective 1 January 2026" (company.md) |

## 2. Economic buyer: founder or CEO who runs payroll

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Job titles | CEO, Co-founder | customer-videos.md: Famasi; customers.md: Cleva |
| Real examples | Adeola (CEO, Famasi); Tolu (Co-founder, Cleva) | customer-videos.md; customers.md |
| Measured on | Implied by research: time, and team paid without stress | customer-videos.md: Famasi ("in entrepreneurship time is very important") |
| Starts looking when | The team grows and manual payroll stops scaling | customer-videos.md: Famasi ("as a team [grew] we realized there was a need for a more sustainable HR solution") |

**Top three pains, own words**
1. "everything was very manual before using paid [PaidHR]"
2. "I had to individually text everyone on the team to confirm that they have received their payment"
3. "I had to air Mark [earmark] about one hour of the day whenever I signed [sat down] to run payroll"

**Likely objections** (from research)
| Objection | Evidence | Response proof |
|---|---|---|
| Other tools looked hard to use | Famasi: "it just didn't seem very simple to use" | Famasi: "it's just a very simple interface to use" |
| Upfront cost | PaidHR removed its onboarding fee to reduce "the barrier to entry" (company.md) | "no upfront fees"; priced per employee per month (company.md) |
| Data security | CTO: "Our clients trust us with their most sensitive workforce data" (company.md) | "ISO 27001 certified and NDPA registered" (company.md) |

## 3. Influencer: employees

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Staff paid through the platform, including shift and offline workers | signals.md: 7 |
| What they want | Pay on time, payslips without asking HR, pension and tax actually remitted, access to earned pay | customer-videos.md; signals.md: 1; company.md: PaidLife |

| Starts looking when | Open question: no employee research |

**Top three pains** (no employee quotes in research; these are PaidHR's and the founder's framing)
1. Founder: "Holding your salary in a currency that loses value by the week is a massive problem." (signals.md: 6)
