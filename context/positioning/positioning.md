## Positioning statement

For growing African businesses that still run payroll by hand or across disconnected tools, PaidHR is the people operations platform that pays staff on time and files statutory remittances automatically, with receipts as proof. Unlike HR tools that stop at records, PaidHR remits tax and pension, is updated for Nigeria's 2026 tax law, and gives employees earned wage access, loans and a wallet at no cost to the employer. 400+ businesses and 25,000+ employees run on it, with ₦934M in PAYE filed in 2025 and zero statutory defaults.
