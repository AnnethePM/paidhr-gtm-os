# PaidHR value proposition by persona

Built only from `context/docs/research/`. Personas from `context/icp/personas.md`.

## Champion: HR operations lead

| | |
|---|---|
| Job to be done | Pay staff and remit statutory deductions on time, with proof, without manual chasing |
| Pain | "remitting statutory deductions"; "two weeks or more"; "calls from the tax office" (customer-videos.md: Heroshe) |
| Gain | "in less than a click I noticed that the payments had gone"; receipts as "evidence for the payment you've made"; "I leave the software to do the operational [tasks] and I do the Strategic task" (customer-videos.md: Heroshe) |
| Proof | Heroshe video; "₦934M in PAYE filed and ₦754M in pension processed in 2025"; "Zero" statutory defaults or delays (company.md: Traction) |

## Economic buyer: founder or CEO

| | |
|---|---|
| Job to be done | Get the team paid correctly every month without spending founder time on it |
| Pain | "everything was very manual"; texting each employee to confirm payment; "about one hour" per payroll run (customer-videos.md: Famasi) |
| Gain | "I have that one hour [saved]"; "rest assured that paid [PaidHR] has my people paid"; "it has made my life simpler" (customer-videos.md: Famasi) |
| Proof | Famasi video; Cleva: "I feel relieved" (customers.md); 400+ businesses and 25,000+ employees (company.md); 95% retention per Accion, June 2025 (customers.md) |

## Influencer: employees

| | |
|---|---|
| Job to be done | Get paid on time, see payslips, and reach earned pay before payday |
| Pain | Waiting for payday; unsure if pension and tax are remitted; salary losing value (signals.md: 1, 6) |
| Gain | "Access to earned salary, before payday."; payslips by self-service; HR via WhatsApp and USSD with "No internet? No problem." (company.md; signals.md: 7) |
| Proof | "N400m+" EWA disbursed and "1500+" active users (company.md: Traction); EWA at "Zero cost to your company" (company.md) |

## Cross-border add-on (any persona with staff abroad)

| | |
|---|---|
| Job to be done | Pay staff in other countries and currencies from one place |
| Pain | "currency fluctuations and regulation"; "juggling between local and Global payroll tools" (customer-videos.md: Cross Border) |
| Gain | One dashboard; employee wallet to convert, spend or save (customer-videos.md) |
| Proof | "$2M+ in cross-border payments processed since October 2024"; 49 currencies (company.md) |
