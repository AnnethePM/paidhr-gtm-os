# Market: regulation and changes that create urgency

Raw research, not conclusions. All sources checked 2026-11-11. Quotes are exact. INFERRED = my reading, not a sourced fact. Not tax advice: verify against the gazetted Acts before using any figure in copy.

## Nigeria: 2025 tax reform

### Effective dates
- Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (NTA) and Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025 (NTAA) both took effect 1 January 2026. Sources: https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/nigeria/insights/2026/FAQs-on-payroll-taxes-and-employer-liability (published 2026-08-28), https://www.safeguardglobal.com/resources/blog/nigeria-tax-act-2025/ (updated 2026-09-28), both checked 2026-11-11
- NTA signed 26 June 2025; it repeals the Personal Income Tax Act. Source: Safeguard Global (above)
- Nigeria Revenue Service (Establishment) Act 2025 creates the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS). Source: Safeguard Global (above). Its effective date: Not found.

### New PAYE bands (annual chargeable income)
Consistent across DLA Piper, Safeguard Global and Legit.ng. Sources: DLA and Safeguard (above), https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/economy/1695169-fg-unveils-timelines-penalties-tax-regime-nigerians (published 2026-02-02, checked 2026-11-11)

| Band | Rate |
|---|---|
| First N800,000 | 0% |
| Next N2,200,000 (to N3m) | 15% |
| Next N9,000,000 (to N12m) | 18% |
| Next N13,000,000 (to N25m) | 21% |
| Next N25,000,000 (to N50m) | 23% |
| Above N50,000,000 | 25% |

- Income at or below the national minimum wage (N70,000 a month) is exempt. Source: DLA Piper (above)

### Reliefs and deductions
- Consolidated Relief Allowance removed. DLA: "the CRA is no longer effective". Source: DLA Piper (above)
- Rent Relief: 20% of annual rent, capped at N500,000 a year, and the employee must prove rent paid (receipt, lease or employer-approved declaration with landlord details). Source: DLA Piper and Safeguard (above)
- National Housing Fund deductions now voluntary for private-sector staff. DLA: employers should "discontinue deductions for National Housing Fund (NHF) contribution, unless explicitly consented to by the employees." Source: DLA Piper (above)
- Pension, health insurance, life insurance, mortgage interest still deductible with written claims. Source: DLA Piper (above)
- Severance tax-free threshold raised from N10m to N50m. Gratuity is taxable and PAYE must be calculated before paying exiting staff. Source: DLA Piper (above)
- Foreign-currency pay is taxed at the naira equivalent at the CBN official rate at time of payment. Source: DLA Piper (above)

### Employer deadlines
- Monthly PAYE remittance: by the 10th of the following month. Sources: Legit.ng, Safeguard (citing Joint Revenue Board 2026 guidelines). CONFLICT: GPA cites NTAA s.107 with "the 21st day of the following month". Source: https://gpa.net/blogs/africa/nigeria-higher-paye-penalties-prompting-tighter-payroll-compliance (published 2026-01-15, checked 2026-11-11)
- Annual employer PAYE return: 31 January for the prior year. Sources: Legit.ng, Safeguard, DLA (above)
- Next annual return deadline: 31 January 2027 (INFERRED from the rule above).

### Penalties
- Failure to deduct: 40% of the amount not deducted. Sources: DLA Piper, Legit.ng, GPA (above)
- Failure to remit: 10% of the unpaid amount plus interest at the CBN rate. Sources: DLA Piper, Legit.ng. CONFLICT: GPA says "10 per cent per annum"; a later GPA post says penalties "compound monthly". Source: https://gpa.net/blogs/africa/nigeria-closing-the-compliance-gap-under-new-tax-regime (published 2026-09-03, checked 2026-11-11)
- Late annual return: N100,000 for the first month, N50,000 for each month after. Sources: DLA Piper, Legit.ng (above)

### Employer liability and enforcement
- DLA: the employer "bears the statutory responsibility for properly deducting PAYE". Source: DLA Piper (above)
- GPA, quoting Lagos IRS commentary: "the reforms now place employers at the centre of PAYE compliance rather than treating them as passive collection agents. Companies with weak payroll controls face a higher risk of sanctions". Source: https://gpa.net/blogs/africa/nigeria-higher-paye-penalties-prompting-tighter-payroll-compliance (checked 2026-11-11)
- A Lagos state tax office sealed "the premises of 10 firms for failing to remit N45.52 million Personal Income Tax of staff" (search summary). Source: exact page not pinned. It came from a web search on 2026-11-11 whose results included https://www.legit.ng/1423916-tax-net-tightens-2026-how-calculate-personal-income-tax-enforcement-begins.html and https://allafrica.com/stories/202601280434.html. Verify before use.
- Lagos IRS "Activates Power of Substitution" under NTAA 2025, collecting from third parties holding defaulters' funds. Source: https://allafrica.com/stories/202601280434.html (headline, checked 2026-11-11)
- DLA: "Payroll systems using the old bands are producing incorrect deductions" (search summary of DLA). Source: DLA Piper (above)
- Unresolved PAYE assessments "will reportedly become final and binding if objections are not raised in time." Source: GPA Sep 2026 (above)
- Who collects PAYE for most employees: state IRS for residents; NRS for non-residents, diplomats and armed forces (search summary). Source: https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/economy/1695169-fg-unveils-timelines-penalties-tax-regime-nigerians (checked 2026-11-11)

### Effect on pay
- January 2026 salaries showed lower PAYE and higher take-home pay (search summary). Source: https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/economy/1695945-nigerian-salary-earners-higher-home-pay-tinubus-tax-reforms-bite (title, checked 2026-11-11)

## Nigeria: minimum wage

- Current national minimum wage: N70,000 a month. Source: https://allafrica.com/stories/202606260042.html (headline "Govt to Review N70,000 Minimum Wage", 2026-06-26, checked 2026-11-11)
- Government (June 2026) said N70,000 "no longer meets current realities" (headline). Source: https://www.arise.tv/gbajabiamila-n70000-minimum-wage-no-longer-meets-current-realities/ (checked 2026-11-11)
- Labour wants talks now; minister says review is due 1 June 2027 (search summary of Oct 2026 reporting). Workers' forum demands N300,000. Sources: https://allafrica.com/stories/202609040021.html , https://saharareporters.com/2026/09/02/nigerian-workers-forum-accuses-tinubu-government-incomplete-n70000-minimum-wage (checked 2026-11-11)
- NSITF: employers contribute 1% of monthly payroll to the Employee Compensation Scheme (search summary). Source: https://www.playroll.com/minimum-wage/nigeria (checked 2026-11-11)
- INFERRED: a new minimum wage would force every Nigerian employer to re-run pay structures and PAYE in one go.

## Kenya: 2026 payroll changes

Mostly vendor blogs, not verified against KRA, NSSF or SHA.
- NSSF: contribution ceiling rose in February 2026, maximum KES 6,480 per party; upper earnings limit KES 108,000 (search summary). Sources: https://thestandard.ke/business/amp/business/article/2001539926/payslips-shrink-as-new-nssf-rates-take-effect , https://afrotools.com/blog/kenya-nssf-contributions-2026/ (checked 2026-11-11)
- SHIF: 2.75% of gross, employee only. Housing Levy: 1.5% employee plus 1.5% employer. Both deductible against PAYE. Source: https://faidihr.com/blog/employee-benefits-statutory-contributions-in-kenya-2026-legal-guide (title, search summary, checked 2026-11-11)
- Deadlines: by the 9th of the following month; one source says the Housing Levy is due by the 9th working day. CONFLICT, unresolved.
