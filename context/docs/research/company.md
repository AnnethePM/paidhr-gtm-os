Source: https://www.paidhr.com/platform/statutory-remittance (checked 2026-11-11)

## Pricing model

- Pricing page headline: "Pricing that works with your needs". Plans: "Standard" ("20 - 100 Employees"), "Enterprise" ("100+ Employees", "Everything in Standard +", "Dedicated support"), "Custom" ("PaidHR suite and more", "Contact Sales"). No prices, currency or free trial shown. Source: https://www.paidhr.com/pricing (checked 2026-11-11)
- "priced per employee per month" and "without steep upfront costs". Source: https://www.accion.org/article/this-hr-platform-drives-growth-for-africas-small-businesses-and-workforce/ (published 2025-06-26, checked 2026-11-11)
- Upfront onboarding fee removed in 2024: "a revised pricing structure that includes no upfront fees." Source: https://thecondia.com/paidhr-removes-upfront-onboarding-fees-paidhiring (published 2024-09-02, checked 2026-11-11)
