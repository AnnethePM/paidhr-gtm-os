## 1. Growth Story feat Heroshe

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1L3R7CMEmjI (checked 2026-11-12)

**Speaker:** Michelle Adaware, at Heroshe. Name spelling from PaidHR's X post (https://x.com/getPaidHR/status/1694649751189283115); the captions say "Michelle Adare". Job title: unclear in the captions ("T4 operations specialist"). INFERRED: probably "HR operations specialist". Check against the video.

**About the customer**
- "we are a service oriented business that deals with cross-border shipping" ... "you can get quality and affordable products from us and UK [US and UK to] Nigeria and Ghana". INFERRED: Heroshe ships from the US and UK to Nigeria and Ghana.

**Problem before PaidHR**
- "the major HR problem or challenge I faced in a couple of years will be remitting statutory deductions"
- "you have to wait for two weeks or more to get that processed and sometimes you keep getting calls from the tax office and honestly could be really overwhelming"
- "most HR software ... they're unable to help most HR professionals process [statutory] deductions which has been a pain to most of us"
- Payslip requests by hand: "every single time when I meet the employees to get their pays ... they always reach out to me and I have to do the manual process"

**What changed**
- Speed: "the first time when I used paid [PaidHR] to remit my salary deductions it was so fast because in less than a click I noticed that the payments had gone"
- Visibility: "I was even able to see like a summary of all of the payments we need to make"
- Proof of payment: "you're able to get your [statutory] deductions remitted and you also gets a receipts" ... "gives you an assurance that you've not just made payment but you've gotten an evidence for the payment you've made"
- Self-service payslips: "with paid [PaidHR] once they get their payments they see their payslip and that body [burden] just goes off my neck"
- Reminders: "I also get notifications for their anniversaries"
- Time for strategic work: "I leave the software to do the operational [tasks] and I do the Strategic task"

**Numbers**
- "two weeks or more" to process statutory remittances before PaidHR.
- Receipts came "in I think the next two days" after remitting.
- No other figures.

**Best quotes for copy** (check against the video first)
- "honestly I think it's superb"
- "I'll recommend pay [PaidHR] to any HR professional if you're a young HR professional or even if you're mid-level experience"

## Patterns across the two case studies (INFERRED)

- Same core pain in both: statutory remittances done by hand, then chasing proof that the payment went through.
- Same proof points: the time each task takes (two weeks for remittances, one hour per payroll run), self-service payslips, and reminders.
- Both speakers are the person who ran payroll themselves: an HR operations lead at Heroshe and the CEO at Famasi.
- Both videos predate the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 and the PaidHR name, so update any quote's framing before using it.
