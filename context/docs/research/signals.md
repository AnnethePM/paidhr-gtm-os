# Signals: what makes a company need PaidHR now

Raw research, not conclusions. All sources checked 2026-11-11. Quotes are exact. INFERRED = my reading, not a sourced fact. Every "why it matters" line is INFERRED unless it carries a source.

## 1. Compliance deadlines and enforcement (Nigeria)

| Signal | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| New PAYE bands live since 1 Jan 2026 | NTA and NTAA took effect 1 January 2026 | https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/nigeria/insights/2026/FAQs-on-payroll-taxes-and-employer-liability |
| Old payroll setups give wrong numbers | DLA: payroll systems on the old bands produce incorrect deductions (search summary) | same |
| Annual PAYE return due 31 January | Late filing: N100,000 first month, N50,000 each month after | https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/economy/1695169-fg-unveils-timelines-penalties-tax-regime-nigerians |
| 40% penalty for failing to deduct | Consistent across DLA, Legit.ng, GPA | https://gpa.net/blogs/africa/nigeria-higher-paye-penalties-prompting-tighter-payroll-compliance |
| Employer is now liable | "employers at the centre of PAYE compliance rather than treating them as passive collection agents" | same GPA page |
| NHF now opt-in | Employers should "discontinue deductions for National Housing Fund (NHF) contribution, unless explicitly consented to by the employees." | DLA Piper (above) |
| Rent relief needs proof per employee | Receipt, lease or declaration with landlord details | DLA Piper (above) |

- INFERRED: the window to 31 January 2027, the first annual return under the new Act, is the clearest time-bound trigger. Companies still on spreadsheets or old software are exposed.
- PaidHR already leans on this: "Tax-law ready" and "Fully updated for Nigeria's new tax law, effective 1 January 2026". Source: https://www.paidhr.com (checked 2026-11-11). Blog: "New Tax Law in Nigeria: What They Mean for Companies, HR and Employees". Source: https://www.paidhr.com/blog/new-tax-law-in-nigeria (title only)
- PaidHR's own past messaging to employees: "Okay? But have your pension and tax been remitted? Do you even know if they'll be remitted at all?" with "Refer us to your employer". Source: https://x.com/getPaidHR/status/1805579953640993214 (2024-06-25, checked 2026-11-11)

## 3. Funding

- PaidHR's named customers are funded startups (Cleva, Famasi Africa). Source: customers.md
- Nigerian startups raised $184.7 million in H1 2026 across 51 deals (search summary). Source: https://nairametrics.com/2026/07/16/nigerian-startups-top-10-attract-132-2-million-capture-71-funding-in-h1-2026/ (checked 2026-11-11)
- One blog argues funding shows up as hiring and tooling spend within two to four quarters. The search summary labelled this the blog's own opinion. Source: https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=10011 (checked 2026-11-11)
- INFERRED signal: a newly announced round, mainly seed to Series A, means a hiring plan and new payroll complexity within months.

## 5. Expansion across borders

- Cross-border is a stated product line: "Pay your global team in any currency, their way." Source: https://www.paidhr.com/platform/global-payroll-employers (checked 2026-11-11)
- "$2M+ in cross-border payments processed since October 2024". Source: https://www.paidhr.com (checked 2026-11-11)
- Founder: "Compliance and cross-border payouts, mostly." (on the core pain). Source: https://www.raenest.com/blog/behind-the-growth-how-paidhr-found-the-right-problem-to-solve (checked 2026-11-11)
- Kenya raised the NSSF ceiling in February 2026 (search summary). Source: https://thestandard.ke/business/amp/business/article/2001539926/payslips-shrink-as-new-nssf-rates-take-effect (checked 2026-11-11)
- INFERRED signals: a Nigerian company opening in Kenya, Ghana or South Africa; staff or contractors paid in USD or GBP; job posts listing locations outside Nigeria.

## 6. Pay pressure and employee financial stress

- Minimum wage review under debate; labour asks up to N300,000 against the current N70,000. Source: https://allafrica.com/stories/202609040021.html (checked 2026-11-11)
- Founder: "Holding your salary in a currency that loses value by the week is a massive problem." Source: Raenest interview (above)
- INFERRED signals: companies with many low- to mid-wage staff (logistics, retail, manufacturing, per Accion's sector list) are prospects for EWA and loans as a retention perk.
