## Main competitors

### SeamlessHR (Nigeria)
- Founded 2017 in Lagos, per CB Insights search summary. Source: https://www.cbinsights.com/company/seamlesshr/alternatives-competitors (checked 2026-11-11)
- H1: "AI-powered HR Management Platform." Source: https://seamlesshr.com (checked 2026-11-11)
- Sub: "Streamline HR with AI-powered software built for office-based and shift-based teams; managing every stage of the employee lifecycle." Source: same.
- Modules: HRMS, Payroll, Performance, Recruitment, Time Management, Employee Benefits; AI assistant "Samira"; payroll financing "only in Nigeria & Kenya". Source: same.
- Markets: Kenya, Uganda, Ghana, Nigeria, South Africa (site selector); offices in Lagos, Nairobi, Accra. Source: same.
- Scale: "Trusted by **1,000+** companies of all sizes." Source: https://seamlesshr.com/pricing/ (checked 2026-11-11). Headline: "SeamlessHR processes ₦1trillion in salaries in 2025". Source: https://nairametrics.com/2026/02/23/seamlesshr-processes-%E2%82%A6-1trillion-in-salaries-in-2025/ (headline only, not fetched)
- Pricing: plans Lite, Core, Advantage, Custom, no prices, "Get a personalised quote." Source: https://seamlesshr.com/pricing/ (checked 2026-11-11)
- Publishes heavy content on the 2025 tax reform, e.g. "Nigeria's 2025 Tax Reforms: Key Payroll Changes for 2026". Source: https://seamlesshr.com/blog/nigerias-2025-tax-reforms-key-payroll-changes-for-2026/ (title, checked 2026-11-11)
- Positioning blog: "We Started with HR. We Never Intended to Stop There". Source: https://seamlesshr.com/blog/we-started-with-hr-we-never-intended-to-stop-there/ (title only)
- INFERRED: the closest like-for-like rival. Bigger (₦1trn vs PaidHR's ₦47.2bn in 2025), also AI-led, also shift-worker focus.

### Workpay (Kenya)
- H1 (two lines): "Workpay: HR & Payroll Management Software for" / "The EOR and Payroll Solution for Africa." Source: https://www.myworkpay.com (checked 2026-11-11)
- Sub: "Payroll, EOR, and Outsourced Payroll Solutions for growing companies in Africa, helping you expand regionally, hire without entities, and stay compliant." Then "Simple. Compliant. Scalable." Source: same.
- Modules: Employer of Record, Outsourced Payroll, Payroll and Self Service HR, Financial Services. Source: same.
- Reach: "Coverage across 54 African countries"; "Disburse salaries in more than 100 local currencies." Source: https://www.myworkpay.com/pricing (checked 2026-11-11)
- Scale (inconsistent on its own site): "1,000+ Companies", "over 400 companies", "2000+ Businesses trust us", "200k+ Employee payroll processed". Sources: https://www.myworkpay.com , https://www.myworkpay.com/pricing (checked 2026-11-11)
- Pricing: "All prices in USD, per month" but only "Request a Quote". Source: https://www.myworkpay.com/pricing (checked 2026-11-11)
- INFERRED: competes with PaidHR on cross-border and multi-country, adds EOR which PaidHR does not list.
