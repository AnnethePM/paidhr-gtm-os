## Tone observations (INFERRED)

- Website and LinkedIn: plain, benefit-led, short claims ("Payroll that never misses payday"). Leans on hard numbers (₦ processed, "Zero" defaults).
- X: playful, Nigerian internet humour and pidgin-adjacent jokes ("ARE YOU TRYING TO CHEAT ON US???" Source: https://x.com/getPaidHR/status/1788629939341631527), wordplay on the name ("Were you paid or were you 𝑷𝒂𝒅𝒆??" Source: https://x.com/getPaidHR/status/1805579953640993214).
- Blue heart emoji 💙 used as a sign-off on most posts.
- Speaks to two audiences at once: employers (control, compliance) and employees (wellbeing, pay on time).
- CEO's personal X (23.8k followers) is mostly personal and pidgin, with few product posts. Source: https://x.com/seyedele via treg (checked 2026-11-11)
