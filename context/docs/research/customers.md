- LinkedIn, current positioning: "For fast-growing and enterprise organizations across Africa that need to run people operations without stitching together disconnected tools." Source: https://www.linkedin.com/company/getpaidhr/ via treg (checked 2026-11-11)
- Homepage hero sub-copy talks of "African enterprises" (paraphrase of page). Source: https://www.paidhr.com (checked 2026-11-11)
- INFERRED: positioning is moving up-market from "SMEs under 200 employees" (2025) to "fast-growing and enterprise" (2026). Worth confirming internally.
- Average customer size, INFERRED from PaidHR's own figures: 25,000+ employees across 400+ businesses works out to about 60 employees per business. That is arithmetic on self-reported numbers, not a sourced figure.
